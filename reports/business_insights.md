\# Business Insights – E-commerce Seller Performance



\## 1. Dataset Overview



\- Total sellers analysed: 3,095

\- Total item revenue: 13,591,643.70

\- Delivered item revenue: 13,221,498.11

\- Average seller-associated review score: 4.00 / 5

\- Average seller-associated on-time delivery rate: 91.58%



\## 2. Revenue Distribution



\- Average seller item revenue: 4,391.48

\- Median seller item revenue: 821.48

\- Highest seller item revenue: 229,472.63



The mean is substantially higher than the median, suggesting that

item revenue is concentrated among a relatively small number of sellers.



\## 3. Delivery Performance



\- Average seller-associated on-time delivery rate: 91.58%

\- Median seller-associated on-time delivery rate: 100%

\- Sellers without delivery data: 125



The difference between the mean and median suggests variation in

delivery performance across sellers.



\## 4. Customer Reviews



\- Average seller-associated review score: 4.00 / 5

\- Sellers without review data: 5



Review scores are associated with orders rather than individual

sellers. Orders involving multiple sellers can share the same review.



\## 5. Business Recommendations



1\. Investigate sellers with consistently low delivery performance.

2\. Examine high-revenue sellers with comparatively low review scores.

3\. Identify practices used by high-performing sellers.

4\. Consider order volume when comparing seller performance.

5\. Monitor delivery and review metrics over time.



\## 6. Methodology and Limitations



\- Revenue represents item price and excludes freight charges.

\- Delivery performance is based on order-level delivery dates.

\- Customer reviews are order-level ratings associated with sellers

&#x20; through their orders.

\- Multi-seller orders can share the same delivery outcome and review.

\- Seller-level averages may be affected by differences in order volume.

\- Sellers without eligible delivery or review records are excluded

&#x20; from the corresponding metric averages.
